Here is the detailed feature list for **PayTrack**, organized by functional modules based on the product overview document:

## 1. Invoicing & Customization

* **Professional Invoice Creation**: Build clean, professional invoices designed specifically for small businesses and service providers without requiring any accounting knowledge.


* **Template Management**: Maintain and switch between multiple invoice templates.


* **Industry Customization**: Tailor invoices to suit the unique requirements of your specific business and industry.


* **Flexible Payment Accounts**: Add multiple payment and bank account details directly to invoices.


* **Discounts & Adjustments**: Apply custom discounts to invoices.


* **Credits & Debits Handling**: Apply credits, debits, and previous transaction adjustments, as well as carry forward customer credits.


* **Flexible Taxes**: Apply user-defined taxes seamlessly to invoice calculations.



## 2. Distribution & Communication

* **Multi-Channel Invoicing**: Send invoices directly to customers via Email and WhatsApp.


* **Automated Reminders**: Follow up on pending balances by sending payment reminders through Email, WhatsApp, and SMS.


* **Receipt Generation**: Generate official payment receipts upon transaction completion.



## 3. Payment & Debt Tracking

* **Invoice Status Tracking**: Monitor both paid and unpaid invoices from a centralized dashboard.


* **Outstanding Balances**: Gain clear visibility into exactly how much money customers owe.


* **Overdue Debt Management**: Track overdue debts and identify accounts that require follow-up.


* **Partial Payments**: Record partial payments against open invoices.


* **Payment History**: View comprehensive customer payment histories.


* **Customer Statements**: Generate detailed customer statements for auditing and communication.



## 4. Inventory Management

* **Optional Lightweight Catalog**: Maintain a lightweight product inventory catalog that operates independently if needed.


* **Invoice-to-Stock Linking**: Link invoice line items directly to inventory products while keeping service-only invoices completely independent of inventory.


* **Automated Stock Updates**: Automatically deduct and update stock levels when an invoice containing stock items is processed.


* **Stock Monitoring & Alerts**: View current inventory stock levels and receive automated low-stock warnings.



## 5. Financial Reporting

* **Simplified Financial Reports**: Generate straightforward financial reports to review business performance without needing specialized accounting expertise.
